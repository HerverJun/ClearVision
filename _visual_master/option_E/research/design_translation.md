# Option E Design Translation

Option E is not an Apple website, macOS imitation, or a white version of current ClearVision. It translates a disciplined product-design standard into ClearVision's verified high-density desktop workflows.

## What Is Borrowed

- Precise typography hierarchy and optical alignment.
- Whitespace that clarifies ownership and sequence rather than reducing information.
- Controlled contrast and a restrained achromatic material system.
- Subtle depth only for real elevation.
- Consistent icon weight and component behavior.
- Quiet confidence: current object, state, and primary action are obvious without decorative emphasis.

## What Is Not Borrowed

- Apple website composition, product marketing, giant headings, or hero imagery.
- macOS traffic-light chrome, Finder/System Settings imitation, consumer preferences layout, or mobile controls.
- Large soft cards, frosted-glass spectacle, full-width translucency, broad gradients, or display typography in controls.
- Feature removal, hidden high-frequency commands, or low-density minimalism.

## ClearVision Translation

- Flow becomes one unified premium instrument with a dominant canvas, one quiet command bar, adaptive Inspector, contextual operator palette, and contextual Preview/ROI/Result workspace.
- AI becomes a two-column engineering review for the existing candidate, plan/build, difference, validation, rehearsal, handoff, history, and recovery evidence.
- Results, Stations, Diagnostics, and Settings use source-list/detail or task/evidence compositions according to their real function.
- Current copy, routes, controls, states, data, save/run authority, and backend boundaries remain unchanged and authoritative.
