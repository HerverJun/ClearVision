# ClearVision Option E Visual Constitution

## Design Direction

**Apple-inspired Premium Engineering: the restraint, material discipline, typography, and finish of an exceptional product team applied to a high-density industrial vision desktop application.**

The physical scene is a senior vision engineer reviewing parameters and evidence for hours on a calibrated Windows workstation. Ambient light is moderate, errors are costly, and every control must remain legible at 1920x1080 and Windows 125% scaling. The design therefore uses precise hierarchy and quiet materials, not consumer-device spectacle.

CURRENT screenshots and current ClearVision code are the functional source of truth. They are not the layout authority. E can fundamentally reorganize navigation, workspaces, panels, and control grouping, but every verified capability keeps a clear and credible entry and no unverified capability may appear.

## Architecture Principles

1. **Unified instrument.** Shell, Flow, AI, Results, Station, Settings, Dialog, Empty, and authority states share one material, type, spacing, icon, and status system.
2. **Primary task by proportion.** Canvas, image, table, evidence, or form becomes dominant according to the page's real task; chrome recedes.
3. **Adaptive inspectors.** Operator library, Inspector, Preview, diagnostics, and supporting configuration appear as refined contextual regions, not permanent equal-width boxes.
4. **Exceptional spacing, industrial density.** Alignment and cadence create calm without sacrificing visible information or adding steps.
5. **Controlled contrast.** Strong contrast belongs to active selection, primary action, current object, and high-risk state. Inactive surfaces remain quiet but readable.
6. **Subtle depth with purpose.** Elevation differentiates command popovers, adaptive inspectors, modal, and media workspaces only.
7. **Familiar behavior.** Buttons, fields, menus, splitters, tables, modal, and status remain desktop-professional and keyboard credible.

## Flow Workspace

- Use one quiet command bar rather than stacked toolbars.
- Give the canvas the largest continuous area, with compact topology and abundant usable negative space.
- Present the verified operator library as a refined contextual palette using real search/category/recent/favorite/compatibility behavior.
- Present the selected-node Inspector as an adaptive right material panel with real parameter groups and advanced disclosure.
- Treat Preview/ROI/Result as a refined contextual media workspace with a large image surface and precise task tools.
- Keep project/version/save/formal run/stop/result/readiness/run-details/status context visible and visually disciplined.
- Use only verified canvas actions. Do not add Fit, Auto Layout, or any command inferred from other products.

## Product-Wide Translation

- Project and operator libraries remain dense tables and indexes, never decorative card grids.
- Overview uses hierarchy and continuous bands for real recent work, environment, and available functions; no invented metrics.
- Results use a source-list/evidence-detail composition with traceability and diagnostics close to the selected run.
- Station and Inspection use calm operational layouts that preserve current status, commands, checks, metrics, logs, and result evidence without becoming dashboards.
- AI becomes a premium engineering review surface for the existing candidate, plan/build, difference, validation, rehearsal, handoff, history, and recovery stages. It is not a generic AI chat product.
- Settings use a refined source list and wide form/workbench with one clear save boundary. Device and communication pages use their verified list/editor/preview/table/log relationships.

## Visual Language

- Color strategy: restrained achromatic and graphite neutrals with ClearVision action/identity colors used sparingly.
- Surfaces: true neutral whites and cool grays in light areas; deep neutral graphite only where focus benefits. No beige, blue-slate wash, or monochromatic navy theme.
- ClearVision cinnabar remains a precise identity signal. It does not replace NG or destructive semantics.
- Typography: Segoe UI Variable / Microsoft YaHei UI / system sans, one family across labels and data; controlled 12-17px product scale; page titles remain compact; letter spacing 0.
- Spacing: carefully tuned 4/8px control rhythm and 12/16/24px structural rhythm; whitespace is functional, never theatrical.
- Radius: 4-8px for controls and real floating surfaces; no 24px+ cards or consumer capsules.
- Depth: crisp border or restrained short shadow, never both as decoration. Limited translucency may support a real overlay only; glassmorphism is forbidden.
- Icons: precise, compact, consistent stroke and optical size; no macOS traffic-light controls or imitation system chrome.
- Motion reference: 140-200ms state transitions with reduced-motion support; generated images remain static references.

## Apple Inspiration Boundary

E borrows typography hierarchy, whitespace discipline, controlled contrast, refined materials, precise iconography, subtle depth, and quiet confidence. It does not copy an Apple website, macOS window chrome, Finder/System Settings layout verbatim, consumer-device cards, giant titles, soft mobile controls, or translucent spectacle.

## Reject Gate

Reject a screen when any condition is true:

1. It invents or removes a function, control, tab, navigation entry, state, or business fact.
2. It is the current ClearVision screenshot with white surfaces, larger spacing, or rounder corners only.
3. It becomes a marketing page, generic SaaS dashboard, macOS imitation, or low-density consumer app.
4. It drifts from E's unified material, typography, spacing, component, and status language.
5. It hides high-frequency engineering functions behind decorative minimalism.

## Master Chain

`E_FLOW_MASTER -> E_AI_MASTER -> E_SETTINGS_MASTER -> E_FULL_SET`

No downstream E screen is generated until its required E master exists. E never references an A, B, C, or D master.
