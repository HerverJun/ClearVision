# ClearVision Visual Constitution C

Option: `C - Modern AI Engineering`  
Scope: Visual reference images only. This document does not authorize frontend implementation.

## Product Authority

The current ClearVision UI, current code, and current contracts are the sole authority for routes, navigation, controls, fields, tabs, panels, actions, states, and business data. Option C may modernize spatial layering, proportion, typography, spacing, surfaces, borders, and interaction emphasis. It may not create, remove, rename, or reinterpret a capability.

Generated copy and numbers are not authoritative. The target is a reconstruction reference for visual language only.

## Design Character

Option C treats ClearVision as a modern AI-assisted engineering workbench:

- cool, clear, and slightly more breathable;
- layered work zones with strong continuity between intent, validation, evidence, and handoff;
- modern without chatbot or consumer SaaS behavior;
- calm enough for long engineering sessions;
- precise enough for deployment in an industrial environment.

## System Language

- Dark neutral frame around cool silver/light-gray work surfaces with selective graphite task zones.
- Layering comes from border, tonal separation, and spacing rather than floating cards.
- Corners stay in the `4-6 px` range.
- Controls use `30-34 px` heights and rows use `36-42 px`, with more whitespace between semantic regions than Option A.
- Typography remains Windows-native, compact, and direct; section titles are modest.
- ClearVision cinnabar `#b6453c` marks active identity/selection.
- Action blue `#166f9f`; OK `#16866f`; NG `#d12f3f`; warning `#9b6a13`.
- AI stages, recovery, and handoff use the same disciplined state grammar as Flow, Results, and devices.

## Composition

- Stable shell and route identity remain visible while task zones are more clearly layered.
- Primary task surfaces receive generous usable area; inspectors remain compact and anchored.
- AI pages emphasize the real clarification/build/validation/handoff/recovery structure, never chat decoration.
- Lists and forms remain dense, with cleaner grouping and slightly broader breathing room.
- Empty/error/running/result states preserve context and communicate the next real action only.
- Dialogs and drawers remain practical overlays tied to the current owner and route.

## Prohibitions

- No new buttons, tabs, modules, navigation, telemetry, dashboards, charts, cards, shortcuts, AI abilities, device abilities, workflow abilities, or data.
- No chatbot bubbles, prompt-gallery cards, AI magic controls, consumption metrics, generic SaaS cards, glass, glow, gradient decoration, or concept-art framing.
- No oversized headings, poster layouts, or ornamental empty space.
- No invented logo, watermark, third-party mark, or additional brand treatment.

## Master Chain

`C_FLOW_MASTER -> C_AI_MASTER -> C_SETTINGS_MASTER`

All Option C local screens may reference only these Option C Masters. A Master contributes style only; the current screenshot remains the functional authority.
