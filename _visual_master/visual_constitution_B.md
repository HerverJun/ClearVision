# ClearVision Visual Constitution B

Option: `B - Premium Industrial`  
Scope: Visual reference images only. This document does not authorize frontend implementation.

## Product Authority

The current ClearVision UI, current code, and current contracts are the sole authority for routes, navigation, controls, fields, tabs, panels, actions, states, and business data. Option B may refine material hierarchy, spacing, layout proportion, typography, borders, and emphasis. It may not create, remove, rename, or reinterpret a capability.

Generated copy and numbers are not authoritative. The target is a reconstruction reference for visual language only.

## Design Character

Option B presents ClearVision as high-end installed industrial software:

- deep, stable, and materially refined;
- confident without theatrical styling;
- layered enough to orient complex work while remaining dense;
- tactile command hierarchy with restrained depth;
- authoritative evidence, device, and status presentation.

## System Language

- Deep graphite and charcoal working surfaces balanced by neutral steel-gray inspectors and data regions.
- Surface hierarchy comes from controlled value shifts, thin borders, and very restrained shadow.
- Corners stay in the `4-6 px` range; no inflated rounding.
- Compact `30-34 px` controls and `34-40 px` rows on a strict `4/8 px` rhythm.
- Typography is sober, slightly stronger in weight hierarchy, and tuned for dark-surface legibility.
- ClearVision cinnabar `#b6453c` is a precise selection/brand accent, never a page wash.
- Action blue `#166f9f`; OK `#16866f`; NG `#d12f3f`; warning `#9b6a13`.
- State uses shape, icon, label, and color together.

## Composition

- The shell feels installed and durable, with a stable top frame and restrained navigation rail.
- Canvas, preview, evidence, and result imagery remain the largest task surfaces.
- Inspectors and forms use stepped surface values to expose dependency without decorative cards.
- Tables remain compact with subtle row bands and deliberate selected/alert states.
- Modal depth is clear but quiet; dimming never hides the engineering context.
- Empty/error/running/result states retain the surrounding layout and command context.

## Prohibitions

- No new buttons, tabs, modules, navigation, telemetry, dashboards, charts, cards, shortcuts, AI abilities, device abilities, workflow abilities, or data.
- No neon, cyberpunk, game HUD, glow, glassmorphism, decorative gradients, cinematic panels, or black-on-black one-note theming.
- No oversized cards, pills, hero headings, or marketing composition.
- No invented logo, watermark, third-party mark, or additional brand treatment.
